# Company Brain OS Pitch

## Problem

Companies lose truth over time. Official documents become outdated while real decisions happen in Slack, meetings, GitHub, Jira, and email. Existing AI knowledge tools mostly search old content; they do not keep the knowledge itself correct.

## Solution

Company Brain OS is a self-healing organizational memory. It continuously compares official knowledge with newer company events, detects contradictions, explains evidence, asks a human for approval, and then turns the approved decision into workflow actions.

## Innovation

- Self-healing knowledge base instead of passive RAG search.
- Evidence-backed conflict detection.
- Human approval before automation.
- Agentic workflow execution after approval.
- Audit trail for enterprise trust.

## Demo

The demo detects a mismatch between an old JWT payment service document and newer OAuth2 engineering decisions. A human approves the update, then Company Brain simulates Jira, Slack, GitHub, and audit actions.

## One-Line Pitch

Company Brain OS is a self-healing organizational memory that keeps enterprise knowledge accurate and turns approved decisions into action.
